How I review

Every product I recommend will go through the same process.

  1. Research the vendor

    I check the company’s track record, pricing, documentation and customer support options.

  2. Test or demo the product

    I use free trials and vendor demos, and note where the product fits and where it does not.

  3. Match it to a buyer

    I describe the business size, team and goals the product suits, so readers can decide for themselves.

  4. Review regularly

    Software changes quickly. I update my reviews when pricing or features change.
